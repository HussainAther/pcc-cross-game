# Pressure Structural Template Protocol

This benchmark asks whether Pressure has a portable **structure**, not a universal scalar.

The prespecified stages are:

1. **Commitment exposure** — public actions increase the amount/stakes of commitment imposed on the interaction.
2. **Response constriction** — that commitment changes or narrows the opponent's strategically viable response distribution.
3. **Strategic consequence** — the exposure has a measurable downstream behavioral/value consequence rather than being aggression for its own sake.

Statuses are read from frozen synthetic evidence only. Missing evidence remains `unresolved`; structural absence is `absent-by-design` or `not-applicable`. No thresholds are retuned for this comparison.
